---
title: "NSF grant awarded for biomimetic acoustic absorption research"
date: 2024-10-15
---

Prof. Méndez Echenagucia is Co-PI on a new NSF award, "Can Irregular Structural Patterns Beat Perfect Lattices? Biomimicry for Optimal Acoustic Absorption", together with Prof. Ed Habtour (UW) and Prof. Mostafa Nouh (University at Buffalo).
