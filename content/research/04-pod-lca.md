Funded by a $3.7M ARPA-E award (HESTIA program), POD | LCA is an open-source Python ecosystem for dynamic Life Cycle Assessment of buildings. Instead of static, one-off carbon accounting, the framework connects parametric building models with open material data, time-dependent grid emissions and end-of-life scenarios — so that designers can compare embodied and operational carbon consequences of design decisions as they are being made. The project also supports research into the tool requirements for assessing novel carbon-storing construction materials.
