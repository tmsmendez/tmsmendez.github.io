High-fidelity vibro-acoustic simulation is far too slow for early-stage design exploration. This project trains convolutional and graph neural networks as surrogate models for the vibro-acoustic behaviour of timber floor slabs and metamaterial arrays, and uses reinforcement-learning recommenders to guide designers through large parametric design spaces. The goal: simulation-quality feedback at the speed of design iteration.
